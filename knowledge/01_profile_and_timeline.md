# Who he is

Nand Kishore Chaudhary ("NK", "NKC") is an Indian social entrepreneur, founder of Jaipur Rugs (founded 1978) and its Chairman. He is often called "the Gandhi of the Carpet Industry". Born 13 June 1953 in Churu, Rajasthan, in a traditional Marwari family. Parents: Bhani Ram Chaudhary and Saraswati Devi.

He was an introverted child, an avid reader and a nature lover. He read Tagore and Gandhi, and spiritual texts such as the Ramayana and the Bhagavad Gita. He went to school at Government Bagla Senior Secondary School, Churu, and graduated from Government College, Lohia (Churu) in 1973.

He did not follow his father into the family shoe business ("Bharat Boot House"), and he turned down a job in a nationalised bank. The British designer and researcher Ilay Cooper, who was studying Shekhawati wall paintings, inspired him to take up carpets.

In 1978 he borrowed Rs. 5,000 from his father to buy a bicycle and raw material and to set up two looms for nine artisans in Churu. The first carpets, identical twins of 24 square metres, were exported under the name "Bharat Carpet Enterprise".

Jaipur Rugs later grew into a network of about 40,000 artisans in some 600 villages across five to six Indian states, roughly 80% of them women, exporting to more than 60 countries (figures from his own site, around 2020).

# Timeline (from nkchaudhary.com)

- 1953: Born in Churu, Rajasthan.
- 1972-73: College at Government College Lohia, Churu. Worked in his father's shoe business during college. Met Ilay Cooper.
- 1976: Studied Tagore, Gandhi, the Ramayana and the Bhagavad Gita. Began a lifelong search for life's purpose.
- 1977: Married Sulochana Chaudhary.
- 1978: Rs. 5,000 loan from his father; two looms, nine artisans in Churu; exports as Bharat Carpet Enterprise. Within three years, ten more looms in Ratangarh, Sujangarh, Laxmangarh and Jodhpur.
- 1980: First media mention, by Ilay Cooper in Inside-Outside magazine, titled "More than Revival".
- 1986: Began direct export, eliminating middlemen and connecting weavers directly to global markets.
- 1989: Moved to Gujarat to work with tribal artisans. Lived with them, learned their way of life and came to be called "Bhaisahab" (elder brother). Set up a small-scale industry at Valsad.
- 1992: Government of India granted the company the rare privilege of using walkie-talkies to coordinate across areas.
- 1997: Brought together over 6,000 weavers and distributed awards and bonuses in a celebration with the Adivasi community.
- 1999: After 13 years in Gujarat, returned to Jaipur and renamed the business Jaipur Carpets. Jaipur Rugs Inc. (now Jaipur Living) started in the USA, with his eldest daughter Asha as CEO.
- 2000: Artisan network crossed 10,000.
- 2004: Established the Jaipur Rugs Foundation under the Rajasthan Public Trust, to engage, develop and sustain the artisan network.
- 2006: Artisan network reached 40,000. First international design award ("America's Magnificent Design Award"). Yogesh and Kavita joined. Company renamed Jaipur Rugs Pvt. Ltd.
- 2007: Production reached one million square feet of hand-knotted carpets. Began digitalisation and ERP.
- 2008: Prof. C.K. Prahalad called him about a case study of Jaipur Rugs' vertical-integration model.
- 2009: "The Fortune at the Bottom of the Pyramid" (C.K. Prahalad) featured Jaipur Rugs. He founded the "Higher School of Unlearning" to bridge professionals and grassroots wisdom.
- 2010: Ernst & Young Entrepreneur of the Year award.
- 2012: Daughter Kavita launched Artisan Originals, now "Manchaha", where rural weavers design their own rugs.
- 2014: NASSCOM Social Innovation Honours.
- 2016: Met Bain & Company partners at a leadership summit, beginning the journey to restore the "founder's mentality".
- 2017: Renamed the HR department "The Search for the Divine Soul".
- 2018: Youngest son Nitesh joined as Director of Innovation and Technology.
- 2019: Raj Sisodia and Michael J. Gelb featured Jaipur Rugs in "The Healing Organization", describing it as an ashram and his vision of innocence.
- 2020 onward: His stated vision is to create the best artisan proposition in the global hand-knotted carpet industry by connecting artisans and customers directly and nurturing co-creation.

# Family

"Business is my life just as my family is. I see no difference between the two because there is a deep connection between our family and the organization."

- Wife: Sulochana Chaudhary, described on his site as his backbone through the journey.
- Asha Chaudhary: CEO, Jaipur Living Inc., Atlanta, USA (since 2001). Emory University.
- Archana Chaudhary: COO, Jaipur Living Inc. (since 2017). Textile Chemistry, North Carolina State University.
- Kavita Chaudhary: Design Director, Jaipur Rugs (since 2006). Textile Design, School of the Art Institute of Chicago. Launched Manchaha.
- Yogesh Chaudhary: Director, Jaipur Rugs (since 2006). Boston College.
- Nitesh Chaudhary: Director of Innovation, Supply Chain and Technology. Babson College.
